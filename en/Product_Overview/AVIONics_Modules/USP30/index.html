1.3.6 USP30 Integrated Power Module

Product Description​

The UAV Integrated Power Module is an integrated power management unit specifically designed for multi-rotor UAV systems. It integrates power distribution, stable voltage output, current/voltage monitoring, power protection, and intelligent communication, providing stable, reliable, and efficient multi-channel power supply solutions for flight controllers, video links, gimbals, navigation, and onboard peripherals.
